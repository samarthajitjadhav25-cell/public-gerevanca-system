About our portal

Public service starts
with listening.

A community-focused platform for reporting local problems, following progress and strengthening the connection between citizens and service teams.

Project introduction

One trusted place to raise a concern.

Public issues can be difficult to report and hard to follow up. The Public Grievance Management System brings the process into one secure portal: citizens share the details, administrators direct reports to the right department, and each update is recorded for the report owner.

Built as a community engineering project, the portal demonstrates how useful digital services can support transparent, responsive local governance.
